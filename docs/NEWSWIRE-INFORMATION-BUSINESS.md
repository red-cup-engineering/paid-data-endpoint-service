# Newswire as a source-qualified information business

Status: commercial operating thesis grounded in observed 2026-10-24 source and seller readbacks; **not a sold, deployed, or legally guaranteed product**. Own this in the existing public-data/Newswire/Market system; no new merchant, catalog, rights registry, or publisher.

## Decision

**Sell reduced cost and uncertainty of a customer's information decision, not mere access to public facts.** The original Newswire converts external observations into stable, revision-aware, source-attributed Living Web packets. One admitted source can support many *different buyer jobs* only when the output contracts, decision utility, source rights and actual fulfillment differ. A different headline, tag or storefront description is not a new SKU.

The original end-to-end line remains: authorized source acquisition and raw immutable evidence → normalization and source edition identity → independent Newsroom acceptance/correction/withdrawal → original Newswire formats, actual data and PROV-O → original immutable snapshot → original x402 seller → verified external accepted delivery → original Market/Shop/Bazaar/external discovery. Do not fork any stage into a product-specific newswire.

## Paying buyer jobs, not broad "data interested" personas

**First commercial wedge — bounded regulatory change decisions.** A real government-affairs or compliance principal delegates to an agent a precise watch: named agency, type, CFR title/part, RIN/docket, jurisdiction and observation interval. The valuable result is *which newly observed or corrected action warrants review, whether an official source reports a closing/effective date, what the original document says, why the result matches, whether the evidence is current and complete, and what is unknown*. Competitive substitutes are manual source watchlists, direct official APIs, Apify-like extraction, and enterprise compliance suites. Displace manual source reconciliation in a **narrow documented workflow**, not Quorum's entire product. Absolutely no guarantee of jurisdictional applicability, legal advice, official legal status, or source-complete deadlines without official edition verification.

**Second wedge — machine-actionable change receipts for specialized agents.** Software vulnerability applicability (source advisory + exact dependency/version), scientific work opportunity (versioned arXiv + observed research usefulness), weather/hazard transitions (source measurement + units/location/time) and public procurement events (published supplier opportunity + real deadline/terms) have higher potential willingness to pay *when a buyer has a specific running Work request*. Each requires its own authorized source qualification but reuses the same observation/edition/Newsroom/packet/query/merchant. Do not make a new 561 merchant or parser for each. Government records are an already-working first supplier, **not** a company-wide commercial specialization.

**Third wedge — enterprise watch, replay and review.** A customer authorizes bounded watch predicates, time window, alert delivery, failure policy and budget. The service sells meaningful **new/changed/withdrawn** observations relative to that customer's acknowledged cursor, including negative evidence, not an unqualified email digest. Charge for actually delivered task-relevant updates or an openly contracted subscription. Do not activate until a genuine paying repeat customer wants this and the original durable consumer cursor/rights contract is received.

## The throughput decision: governed automation, not per-row editorial labor

A 469-event observed source window cannot economically require 469 separate model-generated articles or discretionary human desk approvals. **Original source testimony, normalized data, and our own interpretation are different kinds of claims**. An independently authorized *source-class policy* should allow the original Newsroom to mechanically receive a data-only attribution packet when the exact source, allowed fields, raw byte custody, immutable source edition, schema, provenance, license/terms edition and no disputed/withdrawn source state are qualified. Such a packet says **"this original publisher reported this field on this observed edition"**, not that 561 verified the underlying regulation or grants legal authority to act. The independent publisher/rights office still owns the policy and revocation; the source-fetching actor cannot self-approve or quietly expand categories. Refusals and contestation remain visible, not dropped.

Separately, an **interpreted news report** or high-stakes statement that a document legally applies to a customer requires additional competent evidence, cross-source checking and independent editorial receiving. Never auto-upgrade a structured source observation to a warranted legal conclusion because a source API supplies a date.

Accept automated data-bulletin batches by qualified original rule at the same standard of source evidence; do not ask a general-purpose LLM to "review" 469 metadata objects one by one. Measure the percent auto-admitted, held, retracted, wrongly merged or needing exception review and the actual work/expense per qualified edition. If the original Newsroom cannot receive source-qualified data lots without invented article-writing labor, that **is a structural commercial bottleneck**, not a reason to fabricate cheap storefront SKUs.

## Revenue arithmetic: demand must come first

A $200/day **gross** goal demands 20,000 accepted purchases/day at $0.01, 2,000 at $0.10, 200 at $1, or 40 at $5; it is much easier to author the first hundred endpoints than to acquire these outside buyer calls. A $6,000/30-day subscription gross would require, illustratively, thirty customers paying $200/month — but none of these prices or customer counts is a recorded purchase or WTP estimate. Net seller contribution is lower after processing, retry, source/rights, settlement, refunds and distribution. Avoid the fallacy that 469 new source records imply 469 paid customers or that one automated agent can safely buy itself into revenue.

Each *actual accepted sale* needs a merchant-attributed P&L record:
- settled gross asset/network and value at an evidenced conversion rate, payment/facilitator and network charges, returns/chargebacks, venue fee;
- source procurement/license/API and reuse permissions, bytes and storage, CPU/peak memory, cache age, raw acquisition attempts and retry cost, immutable history;
- original query/transform/inference cost if used, customer-specific delivery and ongoing support/maintenance/refund obligations;
- independently accepted customer output and source freshness/coverage/latency, not merely HTTP 402, synthetic request count or a marketplace listing;
- contribution margin = attributable settled revenue minus *all* attributable variable costs. Unknown costs remain **unknown**, not zero.

Under the current zero-additional-cash constraint, use preexisting free-eligible public-source access, existing infrastructure and no paid upstream subscriptions, advertisements or purchased market-making traffic without separately approved budgets. The risk of misleading official/public-source attribution is a *customer loss and liability risk*, not free.

## Distribution and go-to-market (one seller, several entrances)

- **Original Newswire/Filum:** public canonical evidence, feed, data JSON, PROV-O and unambiguous free demo. This is an acquisition channel and trust proof, not proof of payment or upstream data ownership.
- **Market:** existing Group merchant/priced technical operation and a cross-market buyer dashboard. A buyer can directly purchase only an installed qualified original seller route; preserve source-controlled price, rights, receipt and recovery. The current source declarations must not be conflated with verified deployed 402s.
- **Shop:** human-readable single-seller presentation and supported actual checkout only where the original merchant/PSP entitlement exists; never pretend a book/support payment link buys data.
- **Bazaar:** multi-provider original merchant discovery/purchase; 561's own source remains the original selling endpoint. Neither Bazaar nor another person's storefront is given default rights to receive funds on the seller's behalf.
- **Outside x402 Bazaars and discovery indices:** register a genuine eligible original 402 route with exactly the advertised input/output schema once merchant rights and live receipts are verified. Independent facilitator indexing and successful payment are separately measured. Inexpensive free discovery and examples help agent search; they are not exclusive data licensing.
- **Direct channel for recurring commercial accounts:** a public developer example showing one exact relevant job and two source-linked successful outputs is stronger than a generic "news API" page. Avoid unsolicited customer outreach or API spend without the organizational authorization already required by the original CRM/sales owner.

## Commercial product acceptance and scale rule

A new source/data SKU must earn its place. The minimum source/offer card names: original publisher and governing rights edition, data subject/identity and revision granularity, cadence/known latency, scope/coverage and sample denominators, fields/units/unknowns, original packet/PROV-O URLs, differentiated buyer job and measurable alternative, request/response example, price hypothesis and metering boundary, payer/payee/asset/network, cash variable cost and failure/refund behavior, marketplace eligibility and first real interested customer.

Operate the following decisions, rather than manufacturing many listings:
1. **Supply eligible?** Exact bytes, stable identity, revisions, qualified rights, non-sensitive sovereignty constraints and actual original-source coverage witnessed. Otherwise only private candidate research.
2. **Product genuinely helpful?** Same-input comparison against directly using the source (or incumbent service) measures **buyer time, calls, token use, decision accuracy and successful task result**. If no improvement, do not sell a wrapper.
3. **Economically and legally saleable?** Exact merchant, source rights, authenticated budget, output guarantee and bounded cost; net contribution positive on a real accepted trial, not a price assertion.
4. **Distribution demonstrated?** Original live HTTP result, signed 402 and payment receipt, independent accepted customer, then own Market/Shop and qualified Bazaar/external discoverability.
5. **Expand sources only as justified by observed demand.** Candidate source intake is one typed source declaration and existing adapter, not new country/industry parser and merchant. Add source families when their exact buyer jobs and rights are qualified; otherwise invest in fixing valuable existing observations.

Immediate business-defining receiving lot: take one of the **32 real proposed rules** from the observed window that has a source-returned comment date; independently establish official source link/deadline and a customer's relevant watch predicate; receive the editorial packet and one actual purchaser's bounded deadline-aware result. If we cannot do that, a larger SKU catalog is not the current economic bottleneck.

## First decision packet now technically shaped by actual external evidence

The official API's basic search omitted comment and effective date fields entirely, making the preceding seven-day source purchase candidate little more than a title list. We extended the **existing Federal Register original acquisition** with a `regulatory-decision` source field profile. A genuine [source receiving run on 2026-10-24](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/paid-data-endpoint-service/actions/runs/40140335583) preserved all **469/469** provider-reported records and now captures **180 source-reported comment-close dates**, **63 effective dates**, **324 docket-linked records**, **78 CFR-linked records** and **four correction links**. The HTTP body total for that exact selected-field response was **673,066 bytes**, and source fields are kept in normalized edition identity and structured Newswire candidate packets. A null source-returned deadline is distinct from a deadline field **not requested** by the API query.

Within that observed source set, **100 records** carried a source-returned closing date between 2026-10-24 and 2026-11-24; of **32 proposed rules**, **30** carried a closing date and **15** fell into that same thirty-one-day inclusive date window. These are measurable candidates for a policy agent's watch, *not* legal advice, a claim that all 100 are still accepting comments, or a buyer's evidenced willingness to pay. The application must independently review any source/official-edition distinction before accepting a customer-specific legal deadline.

The original Newswire's existing `queryPublicNewswire` has now been extended, **without a Federal Register-only paid query engine**, for at most four bounded **RFC 6901 structured-data conditions**, where date-only comparisons use exact source-returned civil dates. It supports snapshot-stable bounded `offset` pagination and optional `withData` (at most eight returned data objects at a time), alongside existing source/topic/time, provenance and editorial admission. Thus the expensive-to-agent task **"which accepted, witnessed proposed-rule packets in my subscribed topic have comment deadlines in this window?"** is one deterministic bounded snapshot query rather than repeatedly reading hundreds of abstracts, improvising date extraction and losing provenance. A future indexed storage implementation may be justified by measured 10k-item scans, not assumed necessary now.

An illustrative buyer request, valid against an **independently received original public Newswire snapshot** once it exists, is:

```json
{
  "kinds": ["data-bulletin"],
  "topics": ["federal-register"],
  "where": [
    {"pointer": "/type", "op": "equals", "value": "Proposed Rule"},
    {"pointer": "/commentsCloseOn", "op": "date-on-or-after", "value": "2026-10-24"},
    {"pointer": "/commentsCloseOn", "op": "date-on-or-before", "value": "2026-11-24"}
  ],
  "limit": 8,
  "offset": 0,
  "withData": true,
  "withProvenance": true
}
```

That input is a **source-operation example, not a quoted or buyable product**. The selected outside buyer still needs original merchant authorization, confirmed source rights, and an installed original paid result with actual settlement. `nextOffset` gives the exact same pinned-release continuation; a result's `totalMatched` and `hasMore` prevent pretending a truncated page is a complete watch. Neither query nor marketplace discovery may advertise a legal deadline guarantee or a real price without the original seller's independent authority.
